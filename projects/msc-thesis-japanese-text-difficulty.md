## About

My M.Sc. thesis investigates how well text-difficulty prediction for Japanese generalizes across domains — comparing classical, feature-based machine-learning approaches against transformer-based models, and measuring how each holds up when moved from in-domain data to out-of-domain (for example, exam-style) text.

_This project is not yet public — the thesis is still in progress. It is kept hidden (`published: false`) until release._
